## 1. Where files live
All active company and client records live in the company Shared Drive so the business owns the files rather than individual staff accounts. Personal My Drive folders are used only for private rough drafts before moving work into the Shared Drive.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is restricted to named external recipients with explicit viewer or commenter access, and external users cannot re-share or change permissions. Public "Anyone with the link" sharing is turned off for business folders unless approved by the owner.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff with provisioned company accounts in the Staff organizational unit may sign in, and 2-Step Verification is required for every account. Company data is restricted to approved devices with screen locks enabled, and suspended or former staff accounts lose sign-in access immediately.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Active project, operational, and financial records are kept in the Shared Drive, while deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days before permanent deletion. Specific statutory retention periods for tax and payroll records are to be checked with the owner and accountant before enforcing automated deletion.

## 5. The monthly export
On the first business day of each month, an administrator exports key Shared Drive folders and critical business records into a dated archive stored on the offline encrypted backup drive. The export size and file count are logged, and one sample file is restored and opened to verify the archive is readable.

## 6. Who holds admin
The business owner alone holds the Super Admin role, using a dedicated admin account with 2-Step Verification and printed backup codes stored offline. Daily work is done from a standard non-admin user account, and delegated staff receive only the minimum admin privileges required.

## 7. When someone leaves
When a staff member leaves, their account is suspended immediately, active sign-in sessions and app tokens are revoked, and their My Drive files and calendar ownership are transferred to their manager or the Shared Drive. After verifying that no shared links or workflows broke, the account is archived or removed according to the offboarding checklist.
